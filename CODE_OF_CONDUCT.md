# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want OmniAgent to be a welcoming project for people with different
backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and viewpoints. We pledge to make
participation harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code or design.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction when impact differs from intent.
- Give actionable feedback and explain the reasoning behind it.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy: do not publish other people's logs, credentials, files, or
  personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior, spam, or bad-faith arguments.
- Encouraging unsafe use of the software, such as exposing Full Access mode to
  untrusted users.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any project
space, as well as public spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block participants when
behavior violates this code. Reports should be sent privately to the
maintainers through the repository's contact or moderation channel. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as possible.
